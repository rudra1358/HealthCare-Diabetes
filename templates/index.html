Everyday care

Good health is built
in the everyday.

Diabetes care is not one-size-fits-all. Small, sustainable habits and regular conversations with a healthcare professional can help you make informed choices.

01 / DAILY RHYTHM

Move in a way
that feels good.

Regular movement can support blood sugar and heart health. Start with something you enjoy and build gradually.

↗
02 / NOURISHMENT

Make room for
balanced meals.

Eating a variety of nourishing foods can help. A registered dietitian can help make a plan that fits your life.

↗
03 / PREVENTION

Keep up with
health check-ins.

Ask your care team about screening, blood pressure, cholesterol, and the schedule that makes sense for you.

↗
04 / SUPPORT

Ask questions.
Find your people.

Good care includes emotional wellbeing. Reach out to trusted people or a professional when you need support.

↗
ⓘ These are general wellness ideas, not a treatment plan. Talk with a qualified healthcare professional about your needs.

Descriptive findings reported for the model’s 64,184-record cohort.

01

Diabetes in the dataset

Most records are non-diabetic; the full dataset contains 8.5% positive cases.

Why it mattersClass imbalance makes accuracy alone an incomplete measure of model performance. Precision-recall metrics are useful context.
02

Smoking history & diabetes

Each bar shows the share of diabetes outcomes within that smoking-history group.

Read with careThis dataset view shows association, not cause. Smoking history alone cannot explain an individual’s health.
03

Who is represented?

Counts across the six smoking-history categories recorded in the source data.

Dataset note“No Info” is a missing-history category. The notebook removes these records before training its model.
04

How the selected model performed

CatBoost results reported by the notebook on its held-out test set.

Important contextThe evaluation used records with known smoking history, not every row in the dataset. Recall and precision describe different trade-offs; no score is a guarantee for an individual.
